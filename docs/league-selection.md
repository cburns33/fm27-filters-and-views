# Club market settings

Keep recruitment markets separate from statistical role profiles.

- Record the managed club and division, transfer/wage ceilings and scouting reach.
- Load the role, then add divisions or nations appropriate to your search.
- Check AND/OR grouping so the market group is required alongside every core statistic.
- Check registration, homegrown and work-permit eligibility for shortlisted players.
- Save a club-named working copy and preserve the league-free master.

A player's current division identifies a source market. It does not establish individual quality, availability or affordability.
